About Concepts

Experience from the GCC and India, at Work in Kerala Homes

Concepts began in 2020 as a decor accessories store in Kozhikode and has grown into a one-stop interiors and decor company: design, build and showroom under one roof.

Our management team brings several years of experience on projects across the GCC and India. That experience is what makes our quality, our timelines and our systematic way of working stand out, and it is why clients trust us for honest advice and on-time handovers.

Built in front of you. Wherever your site has space, our carpenters set up and build right there, so you can see the materials, the workmanship and the progress against the timeline in our proposal. Where there's no room, the major work is done in our workshop, and installation and finishing happen at your site.

Every project is our own, small or big. We give each one the same care, and we build relationships that last well beyond a single contract.

How a Project Runs

From Your First Message to Handover

  1. Tell Us About the Space

    Send a WhatsApp message with photos, a floor plan or just an idea. We reply with the right next step.

  2. Meet Us, Then a Free Site Visit

    We start with a one-to-one meeting to understand each other. Within Calicut city limits we then visit your site free of charge to measure and agree on scope and a working budget.

  3. 3D Design & Quotation

    You see the rooms in 3D with an itemised quote, and we revise until it feels right.

  4. Choose Materials at the Showroom

    Pick wallpapers, laminates, mirrors, flooring and lighting with real samples in hand.

  5. Built at Your Site, in Front of You

    Wherever there is space, we set up and build at your site, so you see the materials, the workmanship and the progress against the timeline in our proposal. If there is no room, the major work is done in our workshop, and installation and finishing are done at your site.

  6. Install & Handover

    Our team installs, cleans up and walks you through everything before you move in.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the site visit free?

Yes, within Calicut city limits. We start with a one-to-one meeting to understand each other and your project, and then visit your site free of charge.

Is project consultation free?

Project consultation is one of our professional services, so it carries a fee. We explain what it covers and the charges at our first meeting, before you commit to anything.

How much do interiors cost?

It depends on the size of the space, the materials and the finishes you choose. We prepare a 3D design and an itemised quotation, so you can see exactly what is included before you decide.

Do you build at our site or in a workshop?

Wherever your site has space, our carpenters build right there, so you can see the materials, workmanship and progress against the agreed timeline. If there is no room, the major work is done in our workshop, and installation and finishing are done at your site.

Where is your showroom and when is it open?

In the heart of Calicut, just 500 m from Kozhikode railway station: St Patrick Church Building, near AKG Flyover, Pushpa Junction, Kozhikode 673002. Open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am to 7:30 pm; closed on Sunday.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Calicut and focus on the Malabar region and central Kerala. We also take on projects across Kerala and beyond, depending on the project.

I live in the Gulf or abroad. Can you handle my project in Kerala?

Yes. Planning, selections and approvals can be done over WhatsApp and phone calls, and family members nearby can visit the site and our showroom. Our management team has years of experience in the GCC, so we understand how clients working abroad like to work.

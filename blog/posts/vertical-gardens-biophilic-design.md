Biophilic design means bringing nature into the places we live and work: plants, daylight, natural textures and views. In a city flat or a busy office, a vertical garden is often the simplest way to start.

## Real or artificial?

Live green walls need irrigation, drainage, light and regular care. Artificial vertical gardens need almost none of that and stay green through monsoon and summer alike, which is why most of our clients choose them for balconies, offices and boundary walls.

## Where they work best

- Behind a dining table or under a staircase, as a calm backdrop.
- In a balcony or terrace to screen a neighbouring view.
- In reception areas and salons, where they make a strong first impression.

## Getting the details right

For outdoor walls, ask for UV-stabilised foliage so the colours hold in the sun. Light the wall with a warm grazing light at night. Dust it with a soft brush or rinse it occasionally to keep it fresh.
